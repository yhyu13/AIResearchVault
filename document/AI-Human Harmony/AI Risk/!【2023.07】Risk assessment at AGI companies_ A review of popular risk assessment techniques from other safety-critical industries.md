## Paper:8




1. Title: Risk assessment at AGI companies: (AGI公司的风险评估)

2. Authors: Leonie Koessler, Jonas Schuett (Leonie Koessler, Jonas Schuett)

3. Affiliation: Centre for the Governance of AI (AI治理中心)

4. Keywords: risk assessment, artificial general intelligence (AGI), catastrophic risks, AI, risk management (风险评估，人工智能，人工通用智能，灾难性风险，风险管理)

5. Url: [Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08823v1), Github: None

6. Summary:
  
  - (1): 本文研究背景是AGI公司建立的人工通用智能系统可能存在灾难性风险，需要改进其风险管理实践。
 
  - (2): 过去的方法不足以评估人工智能的灾难性风险。本文借鉴了其他安全关键行业的风险评估技术，并提出了如何将这些技术应用于评估AI的灾难性风险。方法动机充分。
  
  - (3): 本文提出了三种风险识别技术(场景分析、鱼骨图法、风险分类和分类法)，五种风险分析技术(因果关系映射、德尔菲法、交叉冲击分析、蝴蝶结分析、系统论过程分析)和两种风险评估技术(核对表和风险矩阵)。
  
  - (4): 本文的方法在人工通用智能系统的生命周期中提供了风险评估技术的应用示例，并讨论了何时进行风险评估以及如何使用这些技术。通过使用这些技术，AGI公司可以更好地评估人工智能系统的灾难性风险，以支持其目标。
7. Methods:

- (1): The researchers started by selecting risk assessment techniques from a leading standard (IEC 31010:2019) and popular techniques used in other industries like finance, aviation, nuclear, and biolabs. They defined criteria to exclude and prioritize techniques based on factors such as applicability to catastrophic risks, tail risks, routine tasks, and hardware reliability.

- (2): Techniques that could examine complex interactions between events and combine different perspectives were given priority. They included system-theoretic process analysis (STPA) as an additional technique not listed in IEC 31010:2019. The researchers also prioritized qualitative techniques over quantitative ones, but recognized the value of attempting quantification when communicating about risks.

- (3): For risk identification, three promising techniques were identified: scenario analysis, fishbone method, and risk typologies and taxonomies. Scenario analysis involves developing plausible future scenarios and analyzing their implications on risks. Fishbone method helps identify causes and effects of risks using a graphical representation. Risk typologies and taxonomies assist in categorizing and classifying risks.

- (4): The researchers provided examples of how AGI companies could apply scenario analysis to understand the trajectory of AI progress, the "AGI market," and the geopolitical AI landscape. They suggested combining driving forces and analyzing the risks associated with each scenario.

- (5): The benefits of scenario analysis include using existing information, generating a variety of possibilities, and monitoring warning signs. However, the technique should not be relied upon as a prediction of the future.

Note: The methods described here are a summary of the methodology described in the paper. The content has been condensed and presented in a concise academic style, avoiding repetition of the previous summary.





8. Conclusion:

- (1): The significance of this piece of work lies in its contribution to the improvement of risk management practices in AGI companies for assessing catastrophic risks associated with artificial general intelligence systems. It addresses the limitations of existing methods and provides innovative techniques from other safety-critical industries that can be applied to AI risk assessment.

- (2): Innovation point: The authors innovatively select and adapt risk assessment techniques from various industries, including finance, aviation, nuclear, and biolabs, to address the unique challenges of assessing catastrophic risks from AGI. This interdisciplinary approach brings new perspectives and methodologies to the field of AGI risk assessment.

Performance: The article presents a comprehensive review of ten popular risk assessment techniques, discussing their benefits and limitations in the context of AGI companies. Concrete examples are provided to illustrate how these techniques can be applied to assess catastrophic risks from AI. The authors' recommendations provide practical guidance for AGI companies in implementing these techniques effectively.

Workload: The article emphasizes the importance of conducting risk assessments throughout the lifecycle of AGI systems and highlights the relevance of different techniques at different stages. However, the general nature of the suggestions indicates that further research and customization to individual AGI companies' needs are necessary.
