## Paper:121




1. Title: Systematic review of research on artificial intelligence applications in higher education – where are the educators?

2. Authors: Olaf Zawacki-Richter, Victoria I. Marín, Melissa Bond, and Franziska Gouverneur

3. Affiliation: Faculty of Education and Social Sciences, University of Oldenburg, Germany

4. Keywords: Artificial intelligence, Higher education, Machine learning, Intelligent tutoring systems, Systematic review

5. Url: https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-019-0171-0, Github: None

6. Summary:

- (1): This article aims to provide an overview of research on artificial intelligence applications in higher education, as the field has been around for about 30 years, but it is still unclear for educators how to make pedagogical advantage of it on a broader scale, and how it can actually impact teaching and learning meaningfully.

- (2): The past methods in research on artificial intelligence applications in higher education mostly come from Computer Science and STEM, and quantitative methods were the most frequently used in empirical studies. The weak connection to theoretical pedagogical perspectives and the almost lack of critical reflection of challenges and risks of AIEd are the problems with the past methods. The approach in this paper is well motivated as it provides a systematic review of the research on AIEd in higher education.

- (3): The research methodology proposed in this paper is a systematic review. Out of 2656 initially identified publications for the period between 2007 and 2018, 146 articles were included for final synthesis, according to explicit inclusion and exclusion criteria. The synthesis of results presents four areas of AIEd applications in academic support services, and institutional and administrative services: profiling and prediction, assessment and evaluation, adaptive systems and personalisation, and intelligent tutoring systems.

- (4): The methods in this paper do not focus on achieving performance, but rather provide a comprehensive overview of the research on AIEd in higher education. The conclusions reflect on the need for further exploration of ethical and educational approaches in the application of AIEd in higher education.
7. Methods: 

- (1): 本文旨在系统综述人工智能应用于高等教育方面的研究，首先制定了包含明确包含和排除标准的搜索策略，使用EBSCO Education Source, Web of Science 和 Scopus三个数据库作为检索工具，筛选出自2007年至2018年间发表的与高等教育领域人工智能应用相关的，共2656篇英文论文；

- (2): 每个论文的筛选、数据提取和分类都由三名独立的研究员完成，其中前80篇文章进行了一致性测试；对146篇符合标准的论文进行了细致的类型分类，数据提取和综合分析，并将人工智能在高等教育领域的应用分为四个方面：学术支持服务、机构和行政服务、考核和评价、智能辅导系统。

- (3): 本文采用了系统综述这一研究方法，根据明确的标准对2656篇论文进行筛选，最终选择了146篇文章进行综合分析，以达到回答关于人工智能应用于高等教育方面的具体问题的目的。同时，研究方法的合理性和科学性得到了论文的读者的认可。





8. Conclusion:

- (1): 这篇文章的意义在于为高等教育中人工智能应用的研究提供了系统综述，帮助读者了解这一领域的发展和应用，还指出未来在人工智能应用中需要进一步探讨教育伦理和教育理论方面的问题。这对于在高等教育领域使用人工智能技术的决策者和研究者来说是有实际意义的。

- (2): 创新点：该研究使用系统综述的方法来梳理了高等教育领域人工智能应用的最新研究成果，填补了目前研究中对教育理论和伦理问题的忽视。表现：该文章的研究方法合理、科学；综述的结果详尽准确，具有可读性。工作量：文章在筛选、分类、数据提取与综合分析方面的工作量较大，但三名独立研究员的负责完成使研究的结果更为可靠。
