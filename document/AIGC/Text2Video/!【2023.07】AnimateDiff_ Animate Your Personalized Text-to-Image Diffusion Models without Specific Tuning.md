7. Methods:

- (1): The paper introduces Stable Diffusion (SD) as the general text-to-image generator, based on the Latent Diffusion Model (LDM). SD utilizes an autoencoder, implemented as VQ-GAN or VQ-VAE, for the denoising process in the latent space. The training objective of SD follows the vanilla objective proposed in DDPM.
 
- (2): For personalized image generation, the paper mentions two widely used approaches, DreamBooth and LoRA. DreamBooth fine-tunes the model using a rare string as an indicator to represent the target domain, while LoRA fine-tunes the model weights' residuals. LoRA decomposes the weight matrix to reduce computational costs.
 
- (3): The paper focuses on personalized animation and proposes a method to transform a personalized text-to-image model into an animation generator. This is achieved by training a motion modeling module on video datasets. The module is then plugged into the personalized text-to-image model during inference, requiring little or no additional training. This approach retains the knowledge of personalized models and avoids the need for specific tuning.
