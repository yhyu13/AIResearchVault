## Paper:7




1. Title: Towards Concrete and Connected AI Risk (走向具体和相互连接的AI风险)

2. Authors: Boming Xia, Qinghua Lu, Harsha Perera, Liming Zhu, Zhenchang Xing, Yue Liu, Jon Whittle

3. Affiliation: 第一作者的机构：CSIRO的Data61, Sydney, Australia （Australia's Data61中科院计算机科学与技术研究所所属一个国家实验室）

4. Keywords: artificial intelligence, machine learning, risk assessment, impact assessment, responsible AI, risk mitigation (人工智能、机器学习、风险评估、影响评估、负责任的AI、风险缓解)

5. Urls: Paper: [Link](xxx), Github: None (文章：链接，GitHub代码链接：无)

6. Summary: 

- (1): 本文的研究背景是人工智能（AI）的快速发展引发了对AI系统能力的担忧，尤其是对其做出决策和负责任行为的能力。

- (2): 过去的方法中存在的问题是无法对AI风险进行清晰划分和评估，并缺乏明确的相关框架。本文提供了一个详尽的研究，分析了行业、政府和非政府组织发布的16个AI风险评估框架，并指出了现有框架的不足之处。

- (3): 本文提出了一种 AI 风险评估框架的研究方法，通过对16个现有框架的分析，总结出关键特征，并将它们在负责任AI原则、利益相关者、系统生命周期阶段、地理位置、目标领域和评估方法等方面进行了分析。

- (4): 本文针对AI风险评估框架的任务是对现有框架进行全面分析，并指出了不同框架之间的差异点和共同点，并提出改进的方向。文章并未提及具体性能结果或任务，而是旨在为相关利益相关者选择适当的AI风险评估框架提供洞察和指导。
7. Methods:

- (1): The methodology of this article involves a systematic mapping study following the guidelines on conducting systematic literature reviews and mapping studies in software engineering. The study investigates the capabilities and limitations of existing AI risk assessment frameworks.

- (2): To answer the research questions, the authors performed a literature search using various databases and search terms related to artificial intelligence, machine learning, risk assessment, and mitigation. They selected 16 industrial frameworks that have concrete AI risk assessment solutions.

- (3): The research questions focused on the characteristics of the existing AI risk assessment frameworks, such as demographics, addressed RAI principles, stakeholders, scope, and development stages covered. The assessment processes, including inputs, assessment process, and outputs, were also examined.

- (4): The authors adopted Australia's AI ethics principles as the foundation for their study. These principles include HSE well-being, human-centered values, fairness, privacy protection and security, reliability and safety, transparency and explainability, contestability, and accountability. 

- (5): The methodology employed in this study aimed to provide empirical findings and insights on the capabilities and limitations of existing frameworks, in order to develop concrete and connected AI risk assessment (C2AIRA) frameworks.





8. Conclusion:

- (1): The significance of this piece of work lies in the comprehensive analysis and evaluation of existing AI risk assessment frameworks. By identifying the key features and limitations of these frameworks, the authors provide valuable insights for stakeholders in selecting appropriate AI risk assessment frameworks.

- (2): Innovation point: This article innovatively combines systematic mapping study methodology with AI risk assessment frameworks, providing a structured analysis of 16 existing frameworks. 

Performance: The article successfully analyzes the characteristics of the frameworks in terms of responsible AI principles, stakeholders, system lifecycle stages, geographic locations, target domains, and assessment methods.

Workload: The article demonstrates a considerable workload in conducting the systematic mapping study, literature search, and analyzing the 16 frameworks. The authors provide a comprehensive review of the frameworks, highlighting their similarities and differences, as well as proposing directions for improvement.

Overall, this article offers valuable insights into the existing AI risk assessment frameworks and serves as a guide for stakeholders in selecting appropriate frameworks for assessing AI risks.
