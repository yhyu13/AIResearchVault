链接主义和符号主义是两种不同的认知模型，它们对于智能系统的表示和计算有不同的假设[1](https://axon.cs.byu.edu/papers/ventura.iasted96.pdf) [2](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbestechcouncil/2020/09/01/symbolism-versus-connectionism-in-ai-is-there-a-third-way/)。链接主义认为，智能系统是由大量的简单节点组成的复杂网络，这些节点之间通过连接进行信息传递和处理[3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connectionism)。符号主义认为，智能系统是由一些抽象的符号组成的，这些符号可以通过规则和逻辑进行操作和推理[4](https://louiscastricato.wordpress.com/2020/04/08/joint-representations-of-connectionism-vs-symbolism-via-attractor-networks-and-self-attention/)。

这篇论文中提到的四类内部可解释性方法都是基于链接主义的视角，因为它们都是针对深度神经网络的结构和行为进行解释的[2](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbestechcouncil/2020/09/01/symbolism-versus-connectionism-in-ai-is-there-a-third-way/)。深度神经网络是一种典型的链接主义模型，它们由多层的神经元组成，每个神经元都有一些输入、输出和激活函数[3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connectionism)。😊

链接主义并不是说深度神经网络是完全不可解释的，而是说它们的可解释性是基于它们的内部结构和行为的[1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/connectionism/)。链接主义认为，智能系统的知识和能力是分布在大量的节点和连接中的，而不是由一些单独的符号和规则组成的[2](https://exploringyourmind.com/what-is-connectionism/)。因此，要理解链接主义模型的工作原理，就需要分析它们的层级、单元、连接和激活等组成部分，以及它们之间的相互作用[3](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbestechcouncil/2020/09/01/symbolism-versus-connectionism-in-ai-is-there-a-third-way/)。

符号主义的可解释性是基于它们的外部表达和推理的[1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/connectionism/)。符号主义认为，智能系统的知识和能力是由一些抽象的符号和规则组成的，这些符号和规则可以用一些逻辑语言来表示和操作[2](https://exploringyourmind.com/what-is-connectionism/)。因此，要理解符号主义模型的工作原理，就需要分析它们的符号、规则、语言和逻辑等组成部分，以及它们之间的关系[1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/connectionism/)。😊

Background:  
Deep neural networks (DNNs) have achieved remarkable performance in various domains, such as computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. However, the inner workings of DNNs are generally difficult to understand, raising concerns about the safety, fairness, and accountability of using these systems without a rigorous understanding of how they function.

Takeaways:

-   DNNs are often considered as black-box models that lack transparency and explainability.
-   Interpretability is the ability to explain or understand the behavior and decisions of a model, which is crucial for trust, debugging, and improvement of DNNs.

Introduction:  
Interpretability methods aim to provide insights into the inner components of DNNs, such as neurons, layers, weights, gradients, and activations. These methods can help to answer questions such as: What features does a neuron learn? How do different layers interact? What are the most influential parameters? How does a model generalize to unseen data?

Takeaways:

-   Interpretability methods can be categorized into inner and outer methods. Inner methods focus on interpreting the inner structures of DNNs, while outer methods focus on interpreting the input-output behavior of DNNs.
-   Inner methods can be further classified by what part of the network they help to explain: input attribution, neuron attribution, layer attribution, weight attribution, gradient attribution, or activation attribution.
