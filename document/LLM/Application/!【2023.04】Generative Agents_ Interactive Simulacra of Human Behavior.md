Summary:

The paper proposes the use of generative agents as believable proxies of human behavior for more powerful interactive applications. The approach involves natural language interaction, memory recall, and dynamic plan retrieval to create open-ended social behaviors. The use of large language models is suggested as a key ingredient.  
本文提出了使用生成代理人作为可信的代理人的人类行为更强大的交互式应用程序。该方法涉及自然语言交互，记忆回忆和动态计划检索，以创建开放式的社会行为。建议使用大型语言模型作为一个关键因素。

Methods:

The generative agent architecture is introduced, which handles retrieval and dynamically updates past experience mixed with current context and plans. The study conducted controlled and end-to-end evaluations to identify crucial components and the potential use of large language models. Architecture optimizations were made to achieve concise summaries of agent identity information. Agent interview questions were provided as examples.  
生成代理体系结构的介绍，处理检索和动态更新过去的经验与当前的上下文和计划混合。该研究进行了受控和端到端的评估，以确定关键组件和大型语言模型的潜在用途。对体系结构进行了优化，以实现代理身份信息的简明摘要。代理面试问题作为例子提供。

Conclusion:

The paper presents a new approach to creating believable proxies of human behavior and opens up new research directions for human-computer interaction. The use of generative agents and large language models can enhance the creation of interactive applications and open up possibilities for social behaviors.  
本文提出了一种新的方法来创建可信的人类行为的代理，并开辟了新的研究方向，人机交互。使用生成代理和大型语言模型可以增强交互式应用程序的创建，并为社交行为开辟可能性。

**Background** The paper introduces generative agents, computational software that simulates human behavior to enable more powerful interactive applications. Existing approaches for creating believable agents are limited by their ability to incorporate past experience into their behavior.  
背景本文介绍了生成代理，计算软件，模拟人类行为，使更强大的交互式应用程序。现有的方法，创造可信的代理人是有限的，他们的能力，将过去的经验，他们的行为。

**Introduction** The architecture aims to enhance the creation of believable agents that interact dynamically through natural language, remembering and reflecting on past experiences, synthesizing those memories over time into higher-level reflections, and retrieving them dynamically to plan their behavior. Generative agents interact with each other in open-ended ways, allowing for the emergence of social behaviors.  
介绍该架构的目的是提高可信的代理，通过自然语言，记忆和反映过去的经验，随着时间的推移合成这些记忆到更高层次的反射，并动态地检索他们的行为进行动态交互的创建。生成主体以开放的方式相互作用，允许社会行为的出现。

**Related Work** The paper reflects on prior literature in human-AI interaction and situates the agenda of building believable proxies of human behavior within its canon. The paper suggests that large language models can become a key ingredient for creating believable agents.  
相关工作本文反思了人类与人工智能交互的先前文献，并将建立可信的人类行为代理的议程置于其规范之内。该论文认为，大型语言模型可以成为创建可信代理的关键因素。

**Generative Agent Architecture** The approach starts top-down and generates plans recursively by crafting an agent architecture that handles retrieval where past experience is dynamically updated at each time step and mixed with agents’ current context and plans.  
该方法从上到下开始，通过精心设计一个代理架构来递归地生成计划，该代理架构处理检索，其中过去的经验在每个时间步动态更新，并与代理的当前上下文和计划混合。

**Controlled Evaluation** The study conducted a controlled evaluation to identify crucial components of the architecture, and the potential use of large language models. Results showed that generative agents equipped with a complete memory module are capable of recalling past experiences and answering questions in a consistent and believable manner across various contexts.  
受控评估该研究进行了受控评估，以确定架构的关键组件以及大型语言模型的潜在使用。结果表明，配备了完整记忆模块的生成代理能够回忆过去的经验，并在各种背景下以一致和可信的方式回答问题。

**End-to-End Evaluation** The study also conducted an end-to-end evaluation and observed the possible effect of instruction tuning, which seemed to guide the behavior of the agents to be more polite and cooperative overall.  
端到端评估该研究还进行了端到端评估，并观察了指令调整的可能效果，这似乎引导代理的行为总体上更加礼貌和合作。

**Architecture Optimizations** To achieve concise summaries of an agent's identity information, they perform a retrieval and prompt the language model to summarize the descriptors.  
架构优化为了实现代理身份信息的简明摘要，它们执行检索并提示语言模型总结描述符。

**Agent Interview Questions** Example questions were provided to the agents to explore their self-knowledge, typical weekday schedule, and potential reflections with others.  
代理访谈问题向代理提供了示例问题，以探索他们的自我知识，典型的工作日时间表，以及与他人的潜在反思。

Overall, the paper offers an approach to creating believable proxies of human behavior and a new angle for examining foundational human-computer interaction questions around cognitive models, prototyping tools, and ubiquitous computing applications.  
总的来说，本文提供了一种方法来创建可信的代理人的人类行为和一个新的角度来研究基本的人机交互问题的认知模型，原型设计工具和无处不在的计算应用程序。
