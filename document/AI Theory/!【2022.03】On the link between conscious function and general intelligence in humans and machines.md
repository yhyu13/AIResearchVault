## Paper:6




1. Title: On the link between conscious function and general intelligence in humans and machines

2. Authors: Arthur Juliani, Kai Arulkumaran, Shuntaro Sasai, Ryota Kanai

3. Affiliation: Arthur Juliani is affiliated with Microsoft Research, New York City, USA, and Araya Inc., Tokyo, Japan.

4. Keywords: Consciousness, General Intelligence, Cognitive Abilities, Global Workspace Theory, Information Generation Theory, Attention Schema Theory, Artificial Intelligence, Mental Time Travel.

5. Urls: Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05133v2; GitHub: None.

6. Summary:

- (1): This article examines the connection that is often drawn between consciousness in artificial intelligence and its achieving human-level or superhuman level intelligence. The authors explore the role of conscious experience in human and artificial intelligence and its potential applications.
 
- (2): The previous methods did not explicitly relate conscious experience to general intelligence in humans and were unable to provide insights into artificial agents' behavior. The authors believe that incorporating key aspects of cognitive abilities underlying theories of conscious function can significantly improve artificial agents' capabilities in mental time travel. 

- (3): The authors identify three functional theories of conscious function - Global Workspace Theory, Information Generation Theory, and Attention Schema Theory - that relate conscious function to some domain-general intelligence. They show how these theories can be combined into a single implementable model to enhance artificial agents' general intelligence.

- (4): The authors propose a unifying model of mental time travel in humans that incorporates cognitive abilities underlying each of the three functional theories. The proposed model can improve artificial agent's general intelligence, making it a promising near-term goal for AI research. The current state-of-the-art deep learning methods have begun to incorporate key aspects of each of the three functional theories, but they are still far from demonstrating general intelligence. The article offers insights and potential applications of conscious function in humans' and artificial agents' general intelligence.
7. Methods:

- (1): 本文旨在探讨人工智能中的意识体验与人类智能水平或超人类智能水平之间的联系，并研究意识体验在人工智能和人类智能中的作用。作者通过将三种功能理论（全局工作空间理论、信息生成理论和注意力图式理论）融合成一个可操作的模型来增强人工智能的普适智能水平。 

- (2): 通过探索意识体验与人类普适智能之间的关系，本文提出了一种融合了上述三种理论的模型，旨在模拟人类的普适智能行为，并构建人工智能普适智能的模型。 

- (3): 作者将全局工作空间、信息生成和注意力图式三种理论相结合，提出了一个融合模型。该模型涉及到一些认知能力，如知觉、记忆、规划、执行和自我反思等，从而增强人工智能的智能水平。 

- (4): 文章面向人工智能研究界，提供了有关普适智能和意识体验在人工智能中的应用前景和潜在贡献的见解。





8. Conclusion: 

- (1): 本文的意义在于探究了人工智能和人类意识体验之间的关系，并提出了一个将全局工作空间、信息生成和注意力图式三种功能理论相结合的可操作模型，该模型可以提高人工智能的普适智能水平。文章为人工智能领域的研究人员提供了意识体验和普适智能在人工智能中的应用前景和潜在贡献。

- (2): 创新点：本文提出了一个融合思路，将全局工作空间、信息生成和注意力图式三种功能理论相结合成了一个可操作的模型。表现：文章提供了相关函数理论及其实验研究，从意识体验的角度深入探究普适智能的实现方式。工作量：作者阐述了三种功能理论，论文难度较大，需要读者有一定的领域知识储备。
