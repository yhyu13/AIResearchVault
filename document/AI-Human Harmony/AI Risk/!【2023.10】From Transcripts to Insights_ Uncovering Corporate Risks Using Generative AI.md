6. Summary:
- (1): This article aims to explore the value of generative AI tools in uncovering corporate risks.
- (2): The existing methods for measuring risk have limitations, and this approach using generative AI is motivated by the potential of these tools to provide more accurate risk assessments. 
- (3): The research methodology involves developing and validating firm-level risk measures using the GPT 3.5 model. Earnings call transcripts are used as the context for generating risk summaries and assessments.
- (4): The methods proposed in this paper outperform existing risk measures in predicting firm-level volatility and firms' choices, such as investment and innovation. The performance of the AI-based approach supports its usefulness in providing insights into corporate disclosures at a low cost.
7. Methods:

- (1): The authors developed and validated firm-level risk measures using the GPT 3.5 model, a large language model.
 
- (2): Earnings call transcripts were used as the input data for generating risk summaries and assessments. These transcripts provided the contextual information necessary for assessing corporate risks.
 
- (3): The generative AI approach enabled the model to summarize and assess risk information from the transcripts, providing insights into firm-level risk exposures. This approach outperformed existing risk measures in predicting firm-level volatility and firms' choices such as investment and innovation.
