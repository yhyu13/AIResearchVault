1. Title: Evaluating Large Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey (估计大型语言模型：综合调查)

2. Authors: Zishan Guo, Renren Jin, Chuang Liu, Yufei Huang, Dan Shi, Supryadi Linhao Yu, Yan Liu, Jiaxuan Li, Bojian Xiong, Deyi Xiong (郭子山, 金人仁, 刘创, 黄宇飞, 施丹, Supryadi 林浩宇, 刘岩, 李佳轩, 熊伯坚, 熊德毅)

3. Affiliation: Tianjin University (天津大学)

4. Keywords: large language models, evaluation, knowledge and capability evaluation, alignment evaluation, safety evaluation (大型语言模型, 评估, 知识和能力评估, 对齐评估, 安全评估)

5. Urls: None, Github: [https://github.com/tjunlp-lab/Awesome-LLMs-Evaluation-Papers](https://github.com/tjunlp-lab/Awesome-LLMs-Evaluation-Papers)

6. Summary:
- (1): The research background of this article is the increasing use and deployment of large language models (LLMs) across various tasks, leading to concerns about their safety and appropriate usage.
 
- (2): The past methods for evaluating LLMs have been limited and there is a need for a comprehensive evaluation framework. The article highlights the potential risks and AI safety concerns associated with LLMs and motivates the need for a rigorous and comprehensive evaluation approach.

- (3): The research methodology proposed in this paper is a comprehensive survey that categorizes the evaluation of LLMs into three major groups: knowledge and capability evaluation, alignment evaluation, and safety evaluation. The paper provides a review of evaluation methodologies, benchmarks, and specialized domain evaluations. It also discusses the construction of evaluation platforms.

- (4): The methods in this paper focus on evaluating the performance of LLMs in terms of their knowledge and capability, alignment with human values, and safety. The goal is to guide the responsible development of LLMs by maximizing societal benefit while minimizing potential risks. The performance and findings presented in the paper support this goal by providing a comprehensive overview of LLM evaluation.
7. Methods:

- (1): 该论文采用综合调查的方法，将大型语言模型（LLMs）的评估工作分为三个主要类别：知识和能力评估、对齐评估和安全评估。对于每个评估类别，论文提供了评估方法学、基准数据集和专门领域评估的综述，并讨论了评估平台的构建。

- (2): 知识和能力评估主要关注LLMs在知识和能力方面的表现。该部分介绍了通过问答、分类、生成等任务来评估模型的知识和语义理解能力；还包括评估模型的创造性和逻辑推理等方面的能力。

- (3): 对齐评估主要关注LLMs与人类价值观的对齐程度。该部分介绍了使用评估集和基准数据集来衡量模型对人类价值观的理解和表达；还包括了模型生成的样本质量评估以及对冷启动设置的分析。

- (4): 安全评估主要关注LLMs的风险和安全性。该部分介绍了对抗性攻击、隐私保护、不当使用和伦理问题等方面的评估方法和挑战；还讨论了对抗性样本和预训练数据集的处理。

- (5): 该方法旨在指导LLMs的负责开发，既要最大化社会效益，又要最小化潜在风险。通过提供LLMs评估的综合概述，该论文的性能和发现支持实现这一目标。

- (6): 该论文包括了丰富的评估方法学和相关研究的综述，提供了一个全面的LLMs评估框架，并为研究者和开发者提供了指导。同时，该论文还指出了LLMs存在的挑战和未来研究的方向。

- (7): 论文还提供了评估数据集、基准数据集和开源工具的详细介绍，以便其他研究者和开发者能够在此基础上进行更深入的研究和开发工作。





8. Conclusion:

- (1): The significance of this piece of work lies in its comprehensive survey and evaluation of large language models (LLMs). It addresses the increasing concerns about the safety, appropriate usage, and potential risks associated with LLMs. By providing a comprehensive evaluation framework, this work aims to guide the responsible development of LLMs, maximizing societal benefit while minimizing potential risks.

- (2): In terms of innovation point, this article introduces a categorization of LLM evaluation into three major dimensions: knowledge and capability evaluation, alignment evaluation, and safety evaluation. This provides a comprehensive framework for evaluating LLMs. In terms of performance, the article reviews evaluation methodologies and benchmarks, offering insights into the strengths and limitations of current evaluation approaches. In terms of workload, the article provides detailed information on evaluation datasets, benchmarks, and open-source tools, facilitating further research and development in this field. Overall, this work contributes to a better understanding and evaluation of LLMs.
