## Paper:6




1. Title: Communicative Agents for Software Development (与软件开发的通信代理人)

2. Authors: Chen Qian, Xin Cong, Wei Liu, Cheng Yang, Weize Chen, Yusheng Su, Yufan Dang, Jiahao Li, Juyuan Xu, Dahai Li, Zhiyuan Liu, Maosong Sun

3. Affiliation: Tsinghua University (清华大学)

4. Keywords: communicative agents, software development, chat-powered company, large language models (LLMs)

5. Urls: None, Github: https://github.com/OpenBMB/ChatDev

6. Summary:
- (1): This paper addresses the use of communicative agents in software development and the integration of large language models (LLMs) into the process.
- (2): Traditional methods in software development lack effective communication and collaboration, which results in inefficiency and potential errors. The proposed approach of using communicative agents facilitates natural language communication and streamlines the software development process.
- (3): The research methodology involves utilizing a virtual chat-powered company called CHATDEV, which consists of diverse agents collaborating through chat. This company follows the waterfall model and incorporates natural language communication at each stage of software development.
- (4): The methods in this paper achieve remarkable efficacy in software generation, completing the entire software development process in under seven minutes at a cost of less than one dollar. The performance of the proposed approach supports the goals of enhancing efficiency, reducing errors, and integrating LLMs into software development.
7. Methods:

- (1): The research methodology involves the creation of a virtual chat-powered company called CHATDEV, which serves as a platform for collaborative software development.
 
- (2): CHATDEV follows the waterfall model, where the software development process is divided into different stages, including requirements gathering, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance. 

- (3): At each stage, communicative agents are integrated into the process to facilitate natural language communication among team members and improve collaboration efficiency. 

- (4): Large language models (LLMs) are utilized to empower these communicative agents, enabling them to understand and generate code, documentation, and other software artifacts. 

- (5): The performance of this approach is evaluated based on its efficacy in software generation, with remarkable results achieved in completing the entire software development process in under seven minutes at a cost of less than one dollar. 

- (6): The integration of communicative agents and LLMs aims to enhance efficiency, reduce errors, and explore the potential of incorporating natural language communication into software development.





8. Conclusion:

- (1): The significance of this piece of work lies in the development of CHATDEV, a chat-based framework that integrates large language models (LLMs) into software development. It addresses the lack of effective communication and collaboration in traditional software development methods, aiming to enhance efficiency and reduce errors through natural language communication.

- (2): Innovation point: The innovation of this article lies in the use of communicative agents and LLMs to facilitate natural language communication and collaboration in the software development process. This approach streamlines the development process and shows remarkable efficacy in software generation.

Performance: The proposed approach achieves impressive results, being able to complete the entire software development process in under seven minutes at a cost of less than one dollar. This demonstrates the efficiency and cost-effectiveness of the automated software development process driven by CHATDEV.

Workload: The integration of communicative agents and LLMs reduces the workload by automating certain tasks in software development. By utilizing natural language communication, the process becomes more streamlined and efficient, contributing to a reduction in the overall workload.

Overall, this work provides a novel and effective approach to software development by leveraging communicative agents and LLMs. It addresses the limitations of traditional methods and offers potential improvements in efficiency, error reduction, and integration of natural language communication.
